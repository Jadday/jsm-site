In the context of the JSM:

**System** = a documented set of things (objects, ideas, tasks, facts, etc) that aims to solve a problem.

This broad definition allows for almost any problem to be tackled by designing systems against it.

The main advantage of systems is that they are documented. By organising them properly, keeping them updated, and referring to them at the right times, one can really start to develop a 'second brain'. This management of systems is the entire purpose of the JSM.

The next few notes summarise the main types of system used throughout the JSM. Action systems are very personalised and include [[3.1.1 Processes|processes]], [[3.1.2 Trackers|trackers]] and [[3.1.3 Tools|tools]]. Reflection systems are more general and include [[3.1.4 Knowledge|knowledge]], [[3.1.5 Mindsets|mindsets]] and [[3.1.6 Resources|resources]].
