**Mindset** = a set of thoughts.

Mindsets are ways of thinking about a concept. While your actions are technically separate from them, it is much easier and more rewarding to act in accordance with your mind.

Therefore, documenting effective mindsets (such as an approach to eating where you convince yourself that junk food is poison) is an important early step in seeing change, as described further in [[4.3.1 Mindset, Knowledge, Output]].
