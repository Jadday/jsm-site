**Process** = a set of steps.

Processes provide repeatable and clear ways to complete certain tasks, which can be useful when the steps are complex or easy to forget.

Currently, JSM processes are moved into a 'Processes' project in [[Super Productivity|Super Productivity]], so they can be easily added to a day automatically or manually.

Processes are generally static - they don't change often, unless they're being improved. If you find yourself regularly updating or tracking variables in a process, you are probably actually creating a [[3.1.2 Trackers|tracker]].

An additional benefit of processes is that, once you have some in place, adding to or improving them (such as using [Habit Stacking](https://jamesclear.com/habit-stacking)) becomes very easy. Since you already have a habit of following the process, you'll instantly adapt to small changes.
