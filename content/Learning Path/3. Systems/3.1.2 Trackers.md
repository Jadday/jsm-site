**Tracker** = a set of variables.

Trackers are for measuring how things change over time, like diary entries, streaks, water consumption, etc. This ensures daily quotas are hit, keeps up good habits, and allows for trends to be seen and analysed over time.

Currently, JSM trackers are kept in a variety of places. Many will stay within the JSM, but some can live in health apps, gym apps, streak trackers and more.
