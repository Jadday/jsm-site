When it comes to materials or people:

**Fragility** = breaks under stress or change.

**Robustness** = resists stress or change, trying to bring things back to how they were.

**Resilience** = adapts to stress or change, learning how to live within it.

**Antifragility** = innovates under stress or change, leveraging it as a way to improve and produce value.

An antifragile mindset is very powerful for any topic, and you can cultivate it over time. It requires seeing problems and changes as opportunities rather than inconveniences.

For example, let's say you're moving house, and you're going to have a bedroom with a different layout to your previous one. It's quite easy to be resilient to this change - just try to put things in roughly the same place and order they were in before, so your habits adapt quickly, and so you don't accidentally make it worse. However, an antifragile mindset is more likely to analyse the new layout to try and make things more efficient. This may be a big change that takes some getting used to - but it can provide a lot more value in the end.

As another example, let's say you take a train out to a new area in order to take an exam, but you accidentally do so on a day the venue is closed (may or may not be speaking from experience). Robustness in this situation would mean calling the owner, banging on the door, complaining, etc. Resilience would mean readjusting the rest of your schedule for the day, to account for the change. Antifragility would mean using the fact that you've already travelled to create value - you might check what shops are nearby to see if they have anything you're looking for, you might use it as an opportunity to explore the new area if you don't know it well, etc.

Living in an antifragile way means big things will often change in your life. Many ideas will fail, and it may be harder to feel content with how you currently are. But trying again is not starting over. The mindset and knowledge you gain from living like this will lead to huge positive changes over time.
