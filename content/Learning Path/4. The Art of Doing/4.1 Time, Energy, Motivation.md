In order to do something, you need the time, energy and motivation for it. Time is self-explanatory, but the others are more complex.

**Energy** = the physiological (physical body) capacity to do things. 

Even if you want to do something, feeling drained and exhausted will make it very difficult.

In general, energy levels come down to quite a simple formula: sleep, diet and exercise. The system groups in the 'Energy' category allow each of these to be optimised.

**Motivation** = the psychological (mental) capacity to do things.

Even with plenty of time and energy, feeling demotivated makes it almost impossible to get things done.

Motivation is a complex thing, and many factors can influence it, such as how happy you feel, whether you've used dopamine-flooding activities and content, impending deadlines, motivational quotes, and many more.

The most consistent way to have motivation for an activity is to create a [[4.2 Habits|habit]] of doing it.
