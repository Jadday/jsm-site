Consistency is hard to build and easy to break, but it is required in every topic to transform average into excellence. The most difficult part is resisting little temptations in the moment that seem harmless, but actually cause compounding damage.

When you feel weak, and you're tempted to break all your rules, there are lots of motivational quotes and mindsets you could go to in order to bring you back up. But lots of these tell you that you need to be strong, and outwork everyone, and to quit now would be shameful, etc. These messages are overwhelming when you already feel weak, and you're likely to just ignore them.

But there's a quote I love that is so comprehensive, so simple, and so clear. It tells you exactly what you need to do in the gentlest way possible:

"Once you visualise the future you want clearly...

All you need to do is consistently act towards it."

That's all you need to do. One step. Consistently act towards it. If you're following that, 100% success. If not, 100% failure. Then you just ask: "is what I'm about to do acting towards that future?". If the answer is no, then your course of action is clear.
