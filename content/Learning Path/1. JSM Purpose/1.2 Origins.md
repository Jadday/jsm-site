So, how did the JSM come to be?

I have always been a note-taker, writing my ideas, problems and thoughts in digital format. For many years, these existed as disparate pieces of data in loosely categorised folders, reviewed only when I remembered some information from them.

I've tried several ways of organising these notes over the years. I initially worked on a set of "Notes To Live By", which gave advice on motivation, friendship, love and more. I prepared "Operation 20", to help me make good progress by my 20th birthday. And eventually, I created the JSM as a full framework from goals to operations.

However, the original JSM was very different to what exists today. Written as one static document, it mimicked the operating model of a large enterprise, with long strategies, agile ways of working, and regular self-audit processes. Through many different versions over time, it has become more flexible and widely applicable, and its evolution is far from over.
