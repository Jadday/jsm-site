**JSM (Jadday System Manual)** = a framework of systems for supporting effective progress and overall success.

Quite literally, the JSM is Jadday's manual of systems, where Jadday is me, James Daw, the author. We'll get into what a [[3.1 Systems|system]] means, but just know that this is a growing set of things that can help with different parts of life.

The JSM is born from passion, and has become a complex solution through many iterations and experiments. Every individual has complexity in their lives - some in academic subjects, some in social relationships, some in hobbies and arts - and no area of experience is superior to another. Studying productivity is my passion, so the JSM is just a product of my experience.
