The current structure of the JSM is split up into two main sections:

**Learning Path** = content about the nature of the JSM, and advice that applies globally to every area.

**System Groups** = the actual systems of the JSM, grouped into topics and closely linked to one another.

The justification for this structure comes from the trial and error of less effective approaches in the past. A useful but self-contained system, such as a process for going to sleep early, would be much less effective than one paired with knowledge on sleep cycles, a stance on late-night messaging, a streak tracker, and so on. And those supporting systems would be practically useless without the actual process for sleeping early.

After many iterations of focusing on only the most effective systems, this current version aims to augment all of them by linking them together and filling gaps.
